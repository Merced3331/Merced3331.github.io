Album called Touch Starved
I made this collection of songs during the summer of 2025. I wanted to expand my musical talents further and create something that would resonate with people. This album focuses on someone who just got out of a terrible relationship, and even though their partner treated them badly, they still miss the companionship that came with the relationship. Going through the memories all of the pain comes flooding back, but pain isn’t forever.
